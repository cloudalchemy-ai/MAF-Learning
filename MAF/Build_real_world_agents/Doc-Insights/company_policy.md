# Nexus Technologies — Company Policy Handbook

**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Last Updated:** 1 May 2026
**Applies to:** All full-time, part-time, and contract employees of Nexus Technologies Ltd.

## 1. Working Hours and Flexible Working

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30, with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours during which all employees must be available are 10:00 to 15:00.

Employees may request flexible working arrangements after completing their probation period of three months. Requests should be submitted to their line manager and HR at least four weeks in advance. Approved arrangements include compressed hours, staggered start times, and hybrid working.

Hybrid working allows employees to work from home up to three days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated office days where all team members are expected to be on-site. Managers may require additional office days for project-critical periods with two weeks' notice.

Fully remote working is available for roles designated as "remote-eligible" in the job description. Remote employees must be based within the United Kingdom and maintain a stable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps.

## 2. Annual Leave and Public Holidays

All full-time employees are entitled to 28 days of annual leave per calendar year, inclusive of eight UK public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata allocation.

Employees accrue an additional day of annual leave for each complete year of service, up to a maximum of 33 days. Unused leave cannot be carried over into the next calendar year except in exceptional circumstances approved by HR.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for periods of five or more consecutive days. Requests of fewer than five days require at least three working days' notice. Managers should respond to leave requests within two working days.

No more than two team members from the same department may be on annual leave simultaneously unless prior approval is obtained from the department head.

## 3. Sick Leave and Absence

Employees must notify their line manager by 09:30 on the first day of absence due to illness, either by phone or the HR portal. A text message or email to a colleague is not sufficient.

Self-certification is accepted for absences of up to seven consecutive calendar days. For absences exceeding seven days, a fit note from a registered medical practitioner is required.

Full-time employees are entitled to company sick pay of full salary for the first 10 working days of absence in any rolling 12-month period, followed by Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) thereafter. Part-time employees receive company sick pay on a pro-rata basis.

Employees who are absent for more than 20 working days in a rolling 12-month period may be referred to Occupational Health for an assessment. The company reserves the right to request a medical examination by a company-nominated physician.

## 4. Parental Leave

Maternity leave is available for up to 52 weeks. The first 26 weeks are Ordinary Maternity Leave and the remaining 26 weeks are Additional Maternity Leave. The company pays enhanced maternity pay of full salary for the first 16 weeks, followed by Statutory Maternity Pay for weeks 17 to 39.

Paternity leave entitlement is two weeks at full pay, to be taken within 56 days of the birth or adoption. Employees must give at least 15 weeks' notice before the expected week of childbirth.

Shared Parental Leave allows eligible parents to share up to 50 weeks of leave and up to 37 weeks of pay between them. Requests must be submitted at least eight weeks before the first period of shared leave begins.

Adoption leave mirrors the maternity leave policy. The primary adopter receives the same enhanced pay and leave entitlements as maternity leave.

## 5. Expenses and Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 calendar days of being incurred via the company expenses platform. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.

Mileage is reimbursed at HMRC approved rates: 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles and 25p per mile thereafter. Employees must use their own vehicle only when public transport is impractical and must hold valid business insurance.

Hotel accommodation for business travel is capped at £150 per night in London and £100 per night elsewhere in the UK. International accommodation limits are set per destination and published on the intranet.

Meal allowances for business travel are £10 for lunch and £25 for dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Receipts are required for all claims over £5.

Client entertainment expenses require pre-approval from a director for amounts exceeding £100 per event. A brief description of the business purpose and attendees must accompany the claim.

## 6. Code of Conduct and Ethics

All employees are expected to act with integrity, honesty, and professionalism at all times. Nexus Technologies has a zero-tolerance policy towards bribery, corruption, fraud, and harassment.

Employees must not accept gifts or hospitality from clients, suppliers, or partners with a value exceeding £50 without prior written approval from their line manager and the compliance team. All gifts and hospitality must be recorded in the Gifts and Hospitality Register.

Conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing to HR within 14 days of becoming aware of them. This includes outside employment, financial interests in competitors or suppliers, and personal relationships with individuals at client or partner organisations.

Whistleblowing concerns should be raised through the confidential reporting line or directly with the Head of Compliance. The company guarantees protection from retaliation for employees who raise concerns in good faith.

## 7. Data Protection and Information Security

All employees must complete mandatory data protection training within their first month of employment and annually thereafter. Failure to complete training within the required timeframe may result in suspension of system access.

Company data must not be stored on personal devices unless the device is enrolled in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) system. USB drives and external storage devices are prohibited for transferring company data.

Passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters, include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must not repeat any of the previous 12 passwords.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems. Employees must use the company-approved authenticator application. SMS-based authentication is not permitted.

Data breaches or suspected breaches must be reported to the Information Security team within one hour of discovery via the dedicated incident reporting channel. Failure to report a breach promptly is a disciplinary matter.

## 8. IT and Acceptable Use

Company-provided equipment is for business use. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, compromise security, or consume excessive bandwidth.

Installation of unauthorised software on company devices is prohibited. All software requests must be submitted through the IT service desk and approved by the IT security team.

The company monitors network traffic, email communications, and system usage in accordance with its Monitoring Policy. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems.

Use of personal email accounts for business communications is strictly prohibited. All business correspondence must use the company email domain.

Generative AI tools may only be used through company-approved platforms. Employees must not input confidential company data, personal data, or client information into any external AI service. The approved AI tools list is maintained on the intranet and updated quarterly.

## 9. Learning and Development

All employees have an annual learning and development budget of £1,500. This can be used for courses, conferences, certifications, books, and online learning platforms.

Requests for training must be submitted through the L&D portal and approved by the employee's line manager. Training that requires time away from work for more than two consecutive days also needs approval from the department head.

Employees pursuing professional certifications relevant to their role may request additional funding up to £3,000 per certification, subject to a commitment to remain with the company for at least 12 months following certification. If the employee leaves within this period, the certification cost must be repaid on a pro-rata basis.

Study leave of up to five days per year is available for employees sitting professional examinations. Evidence of exam registration must be provided.

## 10. Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures

The company follows a progressive disciplinary process: verbal warning, first written warning, final written warning, and dismissal. Gross misconduct may result in summary dismissal without prior warnings.

Examples of gross misconduct include theft, fraud, physical violence, serious harassment, deliberate damage to company property, serious breach of data protection policies, and being under the influence of drugs or alcohol at work.

Employees subject to disciplinary action have the right to be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative at any formal meeting. A written record of the meeting and its outcome will be provided within five working days.

Grievances should be raised informally with the employee's line manager in the first instance. If the matter cannot be resolved informally, a formal grievance may be submitted in writing to HR. The company aims to hold a formal grievance hearing within 10 working days of receipt.

Appeals against disciplinary or grievance outcomes must be submitted in writing to the HR Director within five working days of receiving the decision. The appeal will be heard by a manager not previously involved in the case.

## 11. Health and Safety

The company is committed to providing a safe working environment in compliance with the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974. All employees have a duty to take reasonable care of their own health and safety and that of others.

Fire evacuation drills are conducted quarterly. All employees must familiarise themselves with the nearest fire exits and assembly points. Fire wardens are identified by high-visibility vests and are responsible for sweeping their designated zones.

Display Screen Equipment (DSE) assessments are available to all employees who use a computer for more than one hour per day. Assessments can be requested through the facilities team and should be repeated annually or when workstation arrangements change.

First aid kits are located in the kitchen area on each floor and in the reception area. Trained first aiders are listed on the noticeboard next to each first aid kit. Any workplace injury or near-miss must be recorded in the accident book within 24 hours.

## 12. Sustainability and Environmental Policy

Nexus Technologies is committed to achieving net-zero carbon emissions by 2035. All employees are encouraged to support this goal through sustainable commuting, reducing waste, and minimising energy consumption.

The cycle-to-work scheme provides interest-free loans of up to £2,000 for the purchase of bicycles and cycling equipment. Electric vehicle salary sacrifice schemes are available for employees on contracts of 12 months or more.

Single-use plastics are banned in all company facilities. The company provides reusable cups, cutlery, and water bottles to all employees. Printing is discouraged; when necessary, double-sided and black-and-white printing should be used.

Business travel by air within the UK is prohibited when a rail alternative of under four hours exists. Carbon offsetting is applied to all international flights booked through the company travel portal.
